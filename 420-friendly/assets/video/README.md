20.5s · 1280×720 · 24fps · h264 · 2.6 MB

A watchable preview of the launch cut, built **entirely from real product
photography** — no generated garments, no models.

Each shot is a slow push-in (1.14× → 1.00×) with 0.45s cross-dissolves,
per-shot colour grading, vignette and grain. Captions are drawn in **frame
space**, not baked into the plate, with a fade envelope that clears them
before each dissolve — otherwise two captions at different zoom levels appear
on screen at once during the transition.

## Relationship to the Raylight edit

This is not the Raylight project's own render. The Raylight MCP exposes no
video export — only `render_stills` / `render_filmstrip`, whose output goes to
the agent, not to disk. The finished Raylight cut (29.1s, with its motion
design and camera work) has to be exported from the Raylight tab by hand.

This preview exists so the cut can be watched without that step. Rebuild it
with `scratchpad/video.py`.
