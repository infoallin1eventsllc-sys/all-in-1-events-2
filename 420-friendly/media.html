OWNER PORTAL

Music & video

Upload a track or a clip and it appears on the front page. No links to copy, nothing to paste.

GOOD TO KNOW

  • • Hide takes something off the site without deleting it. Delete is permanent — there is no second copy.
  • • Click a title to rename it. It saves when you click away.
  • • Only upload music you own or have the rights to use. Putting someone else's song on your shop is the owner's liability, not the platform's.
  • • Prefer a streaming playlist instead? The Sound embeds Spotify, Apple Music and YouTube.
