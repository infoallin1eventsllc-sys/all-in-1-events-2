420 FRIENDLY HQ WALKTHROUGH — NARRATION SCRIPT
Voice: "Julian" (Higgsfield preset, ElevenLabs engine). Times are where each line starts in the film.

0:01  EXTERIOR
This is Four Twenty Friendly headquarters. One building in California, built around four things: a dispensary, a clothing line, a content studio, and an indoor grow. Glass front. Charcoal steel. The badge above the door. Come inside.

0:19  LOBBY
The lobby. Double height, walnut from floor to roof, the logo over a glass bridge. Reception checks every I.D. Twenty-one and over, no exceptions.

0:32  DISPENSARY
Left of the lobby, the dispensary. One long stone counter, lit cases, and a wall of jars behind it. Three menu boards: flower, pre-rolls, edibles. Take a seat by the window and ask a budtender anything.

0:49  VEG ROOM
Through the staff door, cultivation starts. The veg room. Clones and young plants on two tiers, under cool white light, until they are ready to flower.

1:01  FLOWER ROOM
Next door, the flower room. This is where the canopy finishes. Full-spectrum light overhead, fans along the wall. Temperature, humidity and day count sit on the panel by the door. Nothing here is left to guesswork.

1:19  DRY ROOM
Harvest hangs in the dry room. Sixty degrees, sixty percent humidity, two weeks.

1:27  CURE, QUALITY LAB, VAULT
Then it cures in glass. Every batch is checked at the quality lab. Finished stock goes behind the vault door.

1:37  FULFILMENT
Fulfilment. Online clothing orders are packed here, sent down the line, and loaded at the dock. Stock sits on the racking, boxed and counted.

1:48  MERCH FLOOR
Back out front, the merch floor. Archive V twenty-four on the rails. Heavyweight fleece folded on the wall, tees and headwear on the tables. Small runs, no restocks. When it's gone, it's gone.

2:04  STAIR AND BRIDGE (one recording, re-ordered in the edit)
Now up the stair. / Everything downstairs makes the product. Upstairs is where we show it. / From the bridge you see the whole lobby, and the street beyond the glass. / Level two belongs to the content studio.

2:28  CONTENT STUDIO
The content studio. A white stage for video, lit and ready, with a rail of the line beside it. A control desk along the wall. And the podcast set: one table, three seats, the logo behind every conversation. Shoots, drops and episodes all come out of this room.

2:56  EDIT BAY AND BOARDROOM
Last stop. Edit bays for post-production, a lounge for guests, and the boardroom behind glass. Retail, cultivation, studio. One address. Four Twenty Friendly. Embrace the love.
