HOW IT WAS MADE
  Picture   A 3D model of the building written in code (Three.js), rendered
            one frame at a time: 5,790 frames plus a 5-second logo card.
  Voice     "Julian", generated through your Higgsfield account.
  Music     "Chill Rap Beat" by Turning_Pages, from Pixabay, extended to fit.
  Logo      Your own logo file, used at full resolution.
  Hoodies   The chest prints are taken from your four hoodie images and
            wrapped onto 3D display forms.

CHECKS RUN ON THIS FILM
  Every one of the 5,940 frames was scanned for jumps, black frames and
  freezes. Nothing found beyond the intended fades and the closing hold.
  Picture and sound are the same length. Loudness is -16.9 LUFS with a
  peak of -4 dB, a normal level for laptop and meeting-room playback.

LICENCES — WHAT WAS CHECKED
  Music   Pixabay's Content License allows commercial use inside a larger

BEFORE YOU SHOW IT PUBLICLY
  - Listen once start to finish on the MacBook's own speakers.
  - The film describes a cannabis dispensary and is labelled "Concept
    visualization" on the closing card. It is a concept, not a licensed
    site. Keep it apart from marketing for the clothing line.
  - The look is a clean architectural concept model, not photoreal. A
    photoreal version would need the building rebuilt by a 3D artist.

FOR A DEVELOPER (source folder)
  v2.js            The complete scene exactly as rendered.
  v2.html          The page that loads v2.js with the post-processing chain.
  full.js          The interactive version made for the in-chat 3D viewer.
  build.cjs        Applies the film-only changes to full.js to produce v2.js
                   (V2=1 FINAL=1 MERCH=1 INLAY=1 OUT=v2.js node build.cjs).
  merch-patch.js   The hoodie display forms and their prints.
  people-patch.js  The scale figures and where each one stands.
  render-v2.mjs    Steps the scene frame by frame in Chromium (Playwright)
                   and pipes the frames to ffmpeg.
  assemble-v2.sh   Joins the rendered halves, the logo card and the
                   soundtrack into the two video files.
  The scripts contain file paths from the cloud session they ran in. To
  re-render, install Node, Playwright, three@0.181, @fontsource/montserrat
  and ffmpeg, then update those paths.
