# Intake email

Sent after a client books a consultation. Paste into your mail client and
replace the two bracketed bits. Plain text on purpose — it has to survive
Gmail, Outlook and a phone screen, and a plain email from a person reads
better here than a designed one.

The form link: https://claude.ai/code/artifact/fa92200c-73ff-43d2-8ca8-c184107fed7c

---

**Subject:** Before we talk — a few questions about [Business name]

Hi [First name],

Thank you for booking with Meridian Interface. I am looking forward to it.

Before we speak, I would like to understand how your business actually
runs. There is a short form here:

    [form link]

It takes about fifteen minutes. A few notes on it:

1. Skip anything you are unsure about. Six honest answers are worth more
   to me than eighteen guesses, and I would rather ask you about a gap
   than build on something you were not certain of.
2. You can close it and come back — your answers stay in your browser.
3. When you are done, press "Copy my answers" at the bottom and paste
   them into a reply to this email.

Nothing you type is sent anywhere until you email it to me. Please do not
put passwords, card numbers or account logins in it — I never need those,
and if anything like that comes up later I will ask for it separately and
securely.

Once I have your answers I will come back within two working days with
what I would build and what it costs. If I do not think it is worth the
money, I will tell you that instead.

If anything in the form is unclear, call me on (281) 882-9198 and we will
go through it together.
