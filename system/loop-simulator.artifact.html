    What this loop does and does not cover

    Measured against the eight departments a full agency stack claims. Being able to name the gaps is worth more than pretending there are none.

    • Runs Lead capture, follow-up drafting, CRM
    • Runs Content writing from the shared context
    • Runs Measurement, and refusing to over-claim on thin data
    • Runs Self-monitoring and alerting
    • Partial Creative — branded cards only, no photography or video
    • Partial Publishing — adapters built, no channel connected yet
    • Absent Search visibility, reviews and reputation
    • By choice Ad platforms. Publishing costs nothing; ad APIs spend your budget, and that deserves its own decision

    This is a simulation of the mechanism, not a forecast. The schedule, the approval gate, and the thresholds are the real ones — analyze refuses to rank channels under 10 attributed leads, and reports leads_per_post as absent rather than zero when nothing has been published. Lead arrivals are yours to trigger, because predicting them would be the one dishonest thing this page could do.

    The alert threshold is the real one, so it is slow. A backlog is only raised at five or more drafts whose oldest has waited a week — five on one morning is a busy Tuesday, not an incident, and an alert that fires on day one is one nobody reads by day three. That week takes minutes to watch at any speed here, which is why Skip a week exists: the rule stays real and the clock becomes reachable. Lowering the threshold to make the demonstration livelier would misrepresent the one number that decides whether you get interrupted.
