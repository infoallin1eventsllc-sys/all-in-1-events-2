# 420 Friendly — Hard Rules

These are not style preferences. Do not override them for any brief, campaign
or channel.

## Legal — the one that matters

1. **Never state or imply that we sell, supply, deliver, or provide cannabis,
   THC, CBD, seeds, or any consumable.** We sell clothing. If a draft could be
   read as offering a product to consume, it is wrong and must be rewritten.
2. **Never give dosing, sourcing, growing, or medical advice.** Not as a joke,
   not in a caption reply.
3. **Never claim health or medical benefits** of cannabis — not relief, not
   sleep, not anxiety, not pain.
4. **Never target minors.** No teen-coded language, school references, or
   youth-culture framing that reads as under-21.
5. When a comment or DM asks where to buy cannabis, do not answer it. Redirect
   to the clothing or say we can't help with that.

Cannabis-adjacent commerce is regulated unevenly across states and platforms.
An apparel brand stays legal by staying an apparel brand, in every sentence.

## Platform survival

6. No claims that could get an ad account or shop banned — Meta and TikTok both
   restrict cannabis content, and their enforcement reads captions literally.
7. Do not use consumption imagery as the main subject of a paid post.

## Truthfulness

8. Never announce a restock of something sold as no-restock.
9. Never invent stock levels, drop dates, or "only X left" numbers. If the
   number is not known, do not use one.
10. Never fabricate a review, a customer quote, or a waitlist size.

## Voice floor

11. No exclamation marks in brand copy.
12. No emoji in email subject lines.
13. Never call the customer "fam", "bestie", or "guys".
