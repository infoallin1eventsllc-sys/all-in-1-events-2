# 420 Friendly — Positioning

## What this is

An apparel brand. Cannabis-themed heavyweight streetwear, sold in limited runs
that do not restock.

## What it is not

**Not a dispensary. Not a delivery service. Not a seller of cannabis in any
form.** Every product is clothing. This distinction is legal, not stylistic —
see `tone-rules.md`.

## Who it is for

People who are open about cannabis being part of their life and want clothing
that says so without a novelty joke on the front. They buy streetwear already
and judge it on weight, print quality and cut.

## The product argument

- 450gsm fleece — heavier than most of what it sits beside on a rail
- High-density prints, not thin transfers
- Small runs. No restocks. What sells out is gone.
- Members see every drop 24 hours early, with stock held back from the public run

## Why the scarcity is honest

Runs are genuinely small. When we say no restock, there is no restock. The
moment that stops being true the whole voice stops working, so it is a
production commitment before it is a marketing line.

## Competitive position

Not competing on price. Competing on weight and on not being a joke brand. The
category is full of novelty tees; this is streetwear that happens to be
cannabis-themed, and the difference should be visible in the garment.
