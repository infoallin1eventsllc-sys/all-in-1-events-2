# 420 Friendly — Voice

Streetwear brand voice. Confident, short, never trying to convince you.

## How it sounds

Short sentences. Often fragments. The product is good and we say what it is —
we do not sell it twice.

> Heavyweight blackout fleece. High-density prints. Small runs, no restocks.

That is the register: three facts, no adjectives doing emotional work. When a
line needs weight, it comes from the fact (450gsm, 24 hours early, no restocks),
not from an intensifier.

## Vocabulary

Say: drop, run, fleece, print, colorway, the list, members, restock.
Say: "when it's gone, it's gone" — scarcity is real here, not a tactic.

Avoid: "elevate", "curated", "premium", "luxury", "must-have", "game-changer",
"unlock", "level up". Any sentence that could sell a mattress is wrong.

## Rhythm

Lead with the thing. Explain after, if at all.

- Good: "Vibrant Series is live. 450gsm fleece, four pieces, no restock."
- Bad: "We're so excited to finally announce that our brand new Vibrant Series
  collection has officially dropped!"

Exclamation marks: essentially never. The brand does not shout.

## Warmth

"Embrace the love" is the brand's warm register and it is genuine — community,
not a slogan. Use it sparingly enough that it keeps meaning. Warmth shows up as
directness and respect for the reader's time, not as enthusiasm.

## Length

Social captions: one to three lines. Emails: under 120 words. If a drop needs a
paragraph to explain, the drop needs a better name, not more words.
