# All in 1 Events — Approved Lines

## Positioning

- Lighting, photo booth and DJ — one team, one night, one point of contact.
- Nothing falls between vendors when there's only one vendor.
- We're set up before your guests arrive and clear before you're ready to leave.

## Reassurance

- We arrive early. Always.
- Spare gear travels with us — a failure shouldn't reach the floor.
- You'll have one number to call, and it's answered.

## Booking

- Booking now for the season.
- Tell us your date and we'll tell you straight away if it's open.
- Happy to hold a date while you decide.

## First touch

- Thanks for reaching out about [date] — is that confirmed, or still moving?
- What matters most to you for the night?

## Do not use

- "Your special day" — overused to the point of meaninglessness
- "Packages starting at…" as an opener; lead with the date, not the price
- Countdown or false-scarcity urgency. Real dates fill; that is enough.
