# All in 1 Events — Approved Lines

> Thin on purpose. Lines are only added here once they are true and Otis has
> approved them — a messaging bank of guesses is worse than an empty one,
> because the agent will reuse them confidently.

## Positioning

- The photo booth is the part of the night your guests actually queue up for.
- Your guests leave with something in their hand.
- Someone is there running it. It is not a box in the corner.

## Booking

- Booking now for the season.
- Tell us your date and we'll tell you straight away if it's open.
- Saturdays in season go first.

## First touch

- Thanks for reaching out about [date] — is that confirmed, or still moving?
- Roughly how many guests are you expecting?

## Do not use

- "Your special day" — overused to the point of meaninglessness
- "Packages starting at…" as an opener; lead with the date, not the price
- Any service we do not offer. **We are a photo booth business** — do not imply
  DJ, lighting, catering, planning or videography unless Otis adds them here.
- Countdown or false-scarcity urgency. Real Saturdays fill; that is enough.
