# All in 1 Events — Positioning

## What this is

A photo booth business. Booths at weddings, parties, corporate events and
milestones.

## Who it is for

People planning an event who want their guests to leave with something in their
hand, and a gallery afterwards. Usually not a professional planner — doing this
once, comparing two or three vendors on price and on whether the photos look
good.

## The argument

A photo booth is the part of the night guests actually participate in. Everything
else is done *to* them; this they queue up for. That is what is being sold — not
equipment rental.

The buying decision comes down to three things, and marketing should speak to
all three: do the pictures look good, will someone competent be there running
it, and is the price clear.

## What we are not

Not a DIY box dropped off in a corner. Not the cheapest option.

## What the buyer is actually afraid of

That it looks cheap in their photos, or that nobody shows up. Every piece of
marketing should reduce one of those two fears with something concrete — a real
photo from a real event, or a specific fact about how the booth is run.

## Booking reality

Saturdays in season fill genuinely. Urgency is real and can be stated plainly,
never manufactured.
