## Who it is for

Small business owners who need a site that brings in work, and who have been
burned by one of two things: a cheap site that does nothing, or an agency that
would not quote a price without a sales call.

## The argument

Two things, and they are the whole pitch:

1. **The price is published.** A quote you cannot get without a sales call is
   not a price. Saying the number up front is the differentiator, and it only
   works if it stays true.
2. **The site is wired to something.** A site that just sits there is a
   brochure. These are built with the follow-up, the CRM and the content
   pipeline attached — which is the thing most small businesses are missing and
   cannot build themselves.

## What we are not

Not an SEO agency. Not an ad buyer. Not the cheapest.

Say so plainly — refusing work you do not do is a credibility signal in a
category full of people who claim everything.

## What the buyer is actually afraid of

Paying for something they cannot change themselves, and being locked into the
person who built it. Every piece of marketing should push against that:
they can edit it, they own it, they are not trapped.

## Proof available

Meridian builds and runs its own marketing system — this one. That is unusually
strong evidence and should be used: it is not a case study, it is the thing
running the account you are reading.
