# Look study: moving the videos off the navy (12 Nov 2026)

Otis: "this dark blue that we've been using on a lot of the videos, we need to move away
from it... use impeccable... use some connectors, try something different." Four looks,
shown as stills of two beats of the agents film (hook and "You approve"). Free to make;
waiting for his pick before any film is rebuilt.

| Look | Ground | Type | Accent |
|---|---|---|---|
| A Daylight | the website's off-white | ink | brand blue, only on the highlight and the button |
| B Signal Yellow | flat yellow | near-black | black blocks, hard offset shadow |
| C Emerald | deep green | cream | warm yellow |
| D Black Stage | black with a dot grid | white, mono labels | white (pairs with the Monospace Callouts ad) |

What fed it:
- **Impeccable** skill: Persuade mode, "bolder" and "typeset"; no gradients, no glass, one
  idea per frame.
- **Adobe** `suggest_type_palettes` (intents bold/confident/modern): a condensed gothic
  display over a geometric sans (Alternate Gothic + Montserrat). Used League Gothic +
  Montserrat, the open-licence (SIL OFL) equivalents, so nothing needs an Adobe Fonts kit.
- **Raylight** `get_design_guide` craft rules: one idea on screen at a time, UI as a large
  tilted prop with depth, no kicker/headline/subline stack, close on the logo alone.
- No skill named "taste" is in Otis's list.

`looks.html#look=day|signal|emerald|stage&beat=hook|approve`, `node shots.mjs <out_dir>`.
Open question for the chosen look: keep the navy logo light-up ending as the one blue
moment, or make an ending on the new ground.
