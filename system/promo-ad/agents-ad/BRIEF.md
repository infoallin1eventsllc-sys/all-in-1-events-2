# Agents ad: brief for the next build (saved 26 Oct 2026, build not started)

Otis's ask: "an ad that talks about agents, what agents can do for your
business. Digital business cards. Ads, commercials, and video ads. All Meridian
Interface products. Selling a customer. What Meridian offers." Build it in a
later session. Nothing has been generated or spent for it yet.

## What it has to say

Lead with AI agents, then the rest of the lineup, then one call to action.

1. **AI agents** that take work off the owner's plate. Only claim what the
   Meridian system really does (see `system/README.md`): catch every new lead
   from the website into the CRM, write the follow-up, help book the
   appointment, draft replies and posts, send a plain-English weekly report,
   and watch for problems. **Nothing goes out without the owner's OK** (the
   approval queue is enforced in the database). Do not claim the agent talks
   to customers on its own or "works 24/7 answering calls".
2. **Ads, commercials and video ads** for social, websites and email.
3. **Digital business cards**: one tap to call, text, book or save the contact
   (`.claude/skills/meridian-digital-card`).
4. The foundation: **website, online store, booking system, CRM.**
5. CTA: **Book a design appointment at meridianinterface.com**, 281-882-9198.

No prices anywhere. No em dashes in on-screen copy.
