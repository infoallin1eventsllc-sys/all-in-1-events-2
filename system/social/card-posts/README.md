# Digital card pictures for social media (23 Nov 2026)

Stills of Otis's live card (meridianinterface.com/card) on a white plate: 4:5 feed and 9:16 story, with and without the QR code. The QR decodes to https://meridianinterface.com/card (checked with jsQR).

Rebuild: serve a folder with symlinks to the website's public/fonts, brand and card, plus the card built with `--layout 3` as card3/, on port 4840; run `shot.cjs` (390x844 at 3x), then `post.cjs` (needs the two site fonts next to post.html).

A picture in a post is never clickable. Links go in: Instagram bio and story link sticker; Facebook post text, story link sticker and Page action button.
