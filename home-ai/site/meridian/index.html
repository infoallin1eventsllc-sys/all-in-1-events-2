Haven · the AI home agent

Everything the guide said is something Haven does.

Haven runs on a small server in the home and a wall panel in each room you choose. It talks with the family, watches the house, and keeps a rulebook it can’t argue its way around.

  • 01

    Talks and listens

    Push to talk on the panel. It answers in its own voice.

  • 02

    Asks before anything risky

    Unlocking a door, opening the garage or turning the water back on waits for a person’s yes.

  • 03

    Stops leaks

    A wet leak sensor shuts off the water and the water heater, and tells you where.

  • 04

    Learns your comfort

    Say “I’m cold” and it warms the room now, and remembers for that time of day.

  • 05

    Briefs you

    A morning and evening summary: the house, the weather when it’s connected, and anything that needs you.

  • 06

    Shows the whole house

    A live hologram of your own home. Tap a room to fly in and use its controls.
