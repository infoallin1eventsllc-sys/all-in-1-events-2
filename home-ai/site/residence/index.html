Built in: Haven

A home that looks out for you.

Linden House comes with Haven installed: a wall panel in the hall, and one in any room you choose. It knows this house from the plan, and it never touches a lock, the garage or the water without you.

  • Nothing risky without your say-so

    The front door stays locked, the garage stays shut and the water stays off until you say otherwise.

  • A leak is caught, not found

    Sensors under the sink and by the water heater shut off the supply and tell you where.

  • Each room, the way you like it

    Tell it you’re cold and it warms the room now, and remembers for that time of day.

  • The whole house on one panel

    A live hologram of Linden House. Tap a room for its lights, climate and doors.
