    Haven · the AI home agent

    Everything the guide promised is something Haven does.

    Haven runs on a small server in the home and a wall panel in each room you choose. It talks with the family, watches the house and learns what each person likes.

    • Talks and listens

      Push to talk on any panel. It answers in its own voice.

    • Watches the electricity

      Today’s use on the panel, from an energy monitor or clearly marked as an estimate. On your way out, Entry shows what’s still on.

    • Learns your comfort

      Say “I’m cold” and it warms the room now, and remembers it for that time of day.

    • Briefs you

      A morning and evening summary: the house, the forecast once a weather source is connected, and anything that needs you.

    • Goes where you go

      Talk to Haven, run scenes and hear its briefings from an iPhone, an Apple Watch or the car, through Siri.

    • Shows off the model home

      On a model-home panel, Haven runs its scenes on its own between visitors, and stops the moment someone touches the screen.

    Questions

    What people ask before they buy.

    Does it need the internet?

    For conversation, yes. Without it, Haven keeps running its safety rules and automations on the server in the house, still stops a leak, and still understands simple commands like “goodnight”.

    What devices does it work with?

    Haven connects through a Home Assistant hub, so it works with the lights, locks, thermostats, garage doors, water valves and sensors that Home Assistant supports.

    What does it learn about us, and can we delete it?

    Comfort preferences by time of day, likes and dislikes, and routines you’ve accepted. Habits it notices only become suggestions; nothing it learns runs until someone says yes. All of it is listed on the panel under About you, and you can forget one item or everything.

    What leaves the house?

    Conversations go to the AI service that powers Haven’s understanding. If you choose the premium voice, the sentences Haven speaks go to the voice service. Weather requests send only the home’s location. Nothing else leaves.

    Can we change a room’s screen later?

    Yes. Every panel picks its own screen and finish, and the family can change either at any time from the panel itself.
