    Haven · the AI home agent

    Everything the guide promised is something Haven does.

    Haven runs on a small server in the home and a wall panel in each room you choose. It talks with the family, watches the house, learns what people like, and keeps a rulebook it can’t argue its way around.

    • Talks and listens

      Push to talk on any panel, or ask from your phone or watch. It answers in its own voice.

    • Stops a leak in seconds

      A wet sensor shuts off the water and the water heater, and tells you where.

    • Learns your comfort

      Say “I’m cold” and it warms the room now, and remembers it for that time of day.

    • Briefs you

      A morning and evening summary: the house, the forecast once a weather source is connected, and anything that needs you.

    • Shows the whole house

      A live hologram of your own home on the panel. Tap a room to fly in and use its controls.

    • Keeps working offline

      Safety rules and automations run on the server in the house, with or without the internet.

    Questions

    What people ask before they buy.

    Can Haven unlock a door or open the garage on its own?

    No. Unlocking a door, opening the garage and turning the water back on always wait for a person to say yes. The rule lives in the one part of the software that touches hardware, and the AI’s tools have no way around it.

    Does it need the internet?

    For conversation, yes. Without it, Haven keeps running its safety rules and automations on the server in the house, still stops a leak, and still understands simple commands like “goodnight”.

    What devices does it work with?

    Haven connects through a Home Assistant hub, so it works with the lights, locks, thermostats, garage doors, water valves and sensors that Home Assistant supports.

    What does it learn about us, and can we delete it?

    Comfort preferences by time of day, likes and dislikes, and routines you’ve accepted. All of it is listed on the panel under About you, and you can forget one item or everything.

    What leaves the house?

    Conversations go to the AI service that powers Haven’s understanding. If you choose the premium voice, the sentences Haven speaks go to the voice service. Weather requests send only the home’s location. Nothing else leaves.

    Can each room have a different screen and finish?

    Yes. Every panel picks its own screen and finish, and the family can change either later from the panel itself.

    Does it work with an iPhone or Apple Watch?

    Yes, through Siri Shortcuts: talk to Haven, run scenes and get its briefings from your phone, your watch or the car.
