Privacy

Your details, and only for a reply.

This site collects nothing about you unless you send the walkthrough form.

The walkthrough form

When you send it, we receive what you typed: your name, email or phone, company, where the project is, what you are, the voice you picked and your note. It goes to Meridian Interface’s customer records, kept with Supabase, and is used only to reply about your project. We don’t sell it or share it, and we don’t add you to a mailing list.

Everything else

  • No tracking. The site has no analytics, advertising or tracking cookies.
  • Fonts. The typefaces load from Google Fonts, so your browser asks Google for them.
  • The live panel remembers the screen and finish you choose in your own browser’s storage. That never leaves your device.
  • Voice samples and the film play from this site; nothing is recorded.

Seeing or deleting your details

Email otis@meridianinterface.com and we will send you what we hold, correct it or delete it.
