What Haven is

A small server in the home and a wall panel in each room you choose. It talks with the family, watches the house, learns their comfort, and keeps a rulebook it can't argue its way around: unlocking a door, opening the garage or turning the water back on always waits for a person's yes. The hologram on the panel is the home's own plan; any style of home gets one from a short description.
