Shopping by scroll, three ways.

Three samples of the same idea: no product grid, no clicking through. The shopper scrolls, the scents arrive one at a time, and a voice says a line about each over soft music. Nothing here touches the live site.

About the sound. Browsers never play audio until the visitor taps something, so every sample starts silent with a Sound on button. With sound off, the narration still appears as a caption. The voice is a Higgsfield preset called Sloane; the music is the ambient piano written for the hero film. Six products are included to keep the samples short.
