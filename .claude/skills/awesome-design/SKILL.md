# Awesome design, distilled

Twelve principles — one sentence of law, one line of practice. The `taste`
skill holds Otis's specific calls; `meridian-stack` holds his brand tokens;
this is the layer that governs even where neither applies.

**1. Hierarchy is the whole job.** A reader should know what matters most in
half a second. If everything is emphasized, nothing is — one thing per view
gets to be loud.

**2. Contrast creates meaning; sameness creates calm.** Big/small,
dense/empty, mono/prose. Contrast where attention should go, sameness
everywhere else — reversed, the design fails.

**3. Whitespace is structure, not absence.** Space groups what belongs
together and separates what doesn't. Padding that encodes no relationship is
just distance.

**4. Type does 80% of the work.** Right pairing, scale, and spacing look
designed with zero decoration. Tighter and heavier as display grows; breathing
room as body lengthens; labels spaced and capped.

**5. One accent, working.** An accent is a pointing finger — two pointing at
different things is confusion. Semantic state colors are signage, not accent.

**6. The grid is felt, not seen.** Alignment is why pages feel professional
before anyone can say why. Break it once, on purpose, where it earns
attention.

**7. Every element earns its place.** The strongest move is deletion — a
thousand no's per yes. An empty-feeling section is a composition problem, not
a content shortage; never solve it with filler.

**8. Honest beats impressive.** A visible `[PLACEHOLDER]` outranks a
fabricated number. Design that lies — fake stats, invented testimonials,
painted device chrome — is failed design at any polish level.

**9. Motion is punctuation.** One placed reveal is a period; scattered
micro-effects are a stutter. Animate to explain state change, ≤350ms,
respect reduced-motion, cut what says nothing.

**10. Interactive looks interactive; state reads at a glance.** Pills press,
fields accept, cards lift. Loading holds its final footprint. Selection
changes edge, not fill. Nobody hovers to discover.

**11. Copy is a design material.** Words are in the layout whether designed
or not. Concrete beats vibe, short beats clever, buttons label their
consequence.

**12. Consistency compounds; novelty spends.** Reused patterns earn trust;
new ones cost learning. One memorable move per design, funded by ruthless
consistency around it.

## The shipping test

Four questions before anything visual ships — four yeses or it isn't done:

1. **Squint** — does the hierarchy survive blur?
2. **Grayscale** — does it work with color removed?
3. **Phone** — does it hold at 375px?
4. **Stranger** — could someone outside the room say what the page wants
   them to do?
