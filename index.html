Our Story

Made with family, self-love & culture

Secrets of Cint is a Black-owned candle house in San Francisco. Every candle and room spray is hand-poured in small batches — 100% natural soy wax, lead-free cotton wicks, and phthalate-free fragrance — to symbolize family, self-love, and culture, and to encourage the next generation to take creative risks.
