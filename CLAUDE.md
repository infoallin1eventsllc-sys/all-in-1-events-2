# Working rules for this project (Otis Williams, Meridian Interface)

These are Otis's standing instructions, in his words where quoted. They apply to every session.

## Finish one section before starting the next (26 Nov 2026)
"Make sure we complete everything that we say we're going to do before we move to another section of the project. Complete everything on the front. When we're done with the front end completely, then we move to the back."
- Keep a written list of what was promised for the current section and work it to the end. Do not call a section done, or propose the next one, while anything on its list is open.
- Live Cards order: the whole front end first (list in `system/products/live-cards/WHATS-LEFT.md`, "Front end"), then the back end.
- If something on the list truly cannot be done yet (it needs an account, money or a decision of his), say so plainly and name what it is waiting for. That is the only reason to leave it open.

## How he wants things written and shown
- "Put your most important items first and last." (25 Nov 2026)
- Visuals: "I don't want no redundant information, and I want to make it easy for the customer and client to use. I don't want anything to be confusing or they don't know what to do." (25 Nov 2026) The rules that came from this are in the website repo, `docs/LIVE-CARDS-DESIGN.md`, "Say it once".
- Show visuals as a published page he can open (an Artifact), not as files in the chat.

## Where the rest lives
- Session history and current state: `system/SESSION.md`.
- Skills in `.claude/skills/` carry the brand, pre-launch QA, video and digital card rules.
